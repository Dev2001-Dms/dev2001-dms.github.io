Engineering for a Sustainable Agricultural Future

I am an Agricultural Engineer currently pursuing my M.Tech in Farm Machinery and Power Engineering at the renowned Mahamaya College of Agricultural Engineering and Technology (MCAET), Akbarpur, Ambedkar Nagar.
